# Simple Harvest

Right click a fully grown crop to harvest it. The drops go straight into your inventory (anything that does not fit lands at your feet) and the crop is replanted at its first stage. Crops that are not fully grown are left alone.

Works with wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroot, and with modded crops built on the vanilla crop block, such as the More Foods tomatoes, lettuce and onions. Nether wart and cocoa are not crops in this sense and are not harvested.

Minecraft 26.1 to 26.1.2, NeoForge and Fabric (Fabric needs Fabric API).

Build with `./gradlew build`.
